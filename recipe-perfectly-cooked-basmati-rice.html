perfectly cooked basmati rice

ingredients

1 cup
1.5 cups

method

  1. Wash the rice.
  2. Add 1 cup rice and 1.5 cups water to a pot.
  3. Bring to a boil over high heat.
  4. Close the lid and cook on medium heat for 7 minutes.
  5. Open the lid, fluff with a fork, and let it rest.
