egg rice

ingredients

3 cups
3 pcs
1 no
2 cloves
1/2 tsp
1/2 tsp
2 tbsp
1 tbsp
1 tbsp

method

  1. Heat oil in a pan and stir-fry the onion first until softened.
  2. Add the garlic and cook briefly.
  3. Add chilli powder and stir well.
  4. Add the eggs and scramble them in the pan.
  5. Add MSG and soy sauce.
  6. Add the cooked rice and stir-fry until everything is mixed well.
  7. Finish with spring onion and serve.
