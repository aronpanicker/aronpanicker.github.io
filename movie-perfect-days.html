perfect days

year: 2023

director: Wim Wenders

quick thoughts

I do not have much to say about this one, which somehow feels right for the film itself.

Perfect Days is an experience more than a film to explain: the stillness, the routines, the quiet contentment of life, and the different ideas of what happiness can look like.

I loved it, and it feels like the kind of movie I will revisit often.
