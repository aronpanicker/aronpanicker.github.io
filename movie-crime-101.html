movie review: crime 101

year: 2026

director: Bart Layton

quick thoughts

I had already seen a lot of buzz around Crime 101 as a film working in the same territory as Heat, and I ended up loving it.

These are the kinds of tropes I do not mind seeing repeated, as long as the filmmakers know how to execute them well. When that part works, the thrills still land.

The tone here is subdued and quiet, and the characters feel the same way. That restraint gives the film its mood. Even the 101 highway itself starts to feel like a character in the story.
