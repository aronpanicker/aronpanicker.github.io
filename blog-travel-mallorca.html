travel notes: mallorca

date: 2026-02-09

category: travel

quick thoughts

This was a trip planned in just a few days, and we knew it would be the off-season because it was December. Even so, it turned out to be a peaceful time to visit Mallorca.

Palma did not seem to be in a hurry. The beaches were empty, the streets carried a cold breeze, and it was nothing like the bright, sunny beach holiday people usually expect. I did not mind that at all. I have had enough of that kind of beach atmosphere back home in Kerala and in places like Goa, so this quieter version of the island felt different in a good way. The city had the energy of Christmas approaching.

I kept thinking about how clearly the infrastructure and the surroundings were built for huge summer crowds, yet everything was almost empty. Every beach felt deserted. That contrast stayed with me throughout the trip.

Still, compared to the freezing cold in Linz, Mallorca had just enough warmth to feel welcoming. It was a very good beginning to the Christmas holidays. Until next time, Mallorca. I would love to see you again in your prime, in summer.
