no other choice

original title: 어쩔수가없다

year: 2025

director: Park Chan-wook

quick thoughts

Park Chan-wook knows how to go crazy with his characters, and the story itself is so much fun.

Adapted from The Ax by Donald Westlake, it is funny and also feels like a mirror to the current job market. I will definitely be reading the book now.

Maybe it is a tad too long, and it took me a little time to get into it.

This type of cinematography, especially the nature scenes, is something I only seem to see in Korean movies. It feels like a painting.

minimal by design. 1mb club mindset.
