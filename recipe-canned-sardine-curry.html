method

  1. Saute the chopped onion in oil. Add the slit green chillies and curry leaves, then saute the ginger-garlic paste as well.
  2. Turn the flame to the lowest setting. Add chilli powder, turmeric powder, and coriander powder, then saute for 1 minute.
  3. Add the chopped tomato and stir for 1 minute.
  4. Add hot water and bring it to a boil.
  5. Drain the oil from the canned sardines, add them to the curry, and let it simmer.
  6. Turn off the flame and add some coconut milk.
  7. Serve hot with rice.
