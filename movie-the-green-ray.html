the green ray

original title: Le Rayon vert

year: 1986

director: Éric Rohmer

quick thoughts

My first of many Eric Rohmer films, I think.

Marie Riviere is so good here. The moments when Delphine breaks down feel crucial, because they reveal her character layer by layer.

The film captures the feeling of seeking something that is not yet articulate or clearly defined, but still aches inside you. It asks whether the thing you are searching for will really fill whatever absence you feel. It takes a great storyteller to convey the things that cannot really be put into words, and Rohmer does that beautifully here.

It also understands how even well-meaning people can only offer direct solutions to someone who is stuck. Sometimes we need to be stuck. Sometimes we need to sit in the rut and accept that not everything is going to be all right right away. There is something deeply honest in the film's acceptance of that journey.
