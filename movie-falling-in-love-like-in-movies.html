falling in love like in movies

original title: Jatuh Cinta Seperti di Film-Film

year: 2023

director: Yandy Laurens

quick thoughts

My first of many Indonesian movies, I believe.

One of the best examples of nonlinear storytelling I have seen, and it is also a movie about movies, so that is a win-win.

The acting by both leads is the heart of the movie.

The title is as literal as it gets, and as someone who grew up with Indian movies and how love is depicted in them, this really worked for me.

It is meta in the way it shows the real reactions and expectations that come with falling in love with someone. It feels like a reflection on why we love love stories, while also staying with the real side of love, the expectations, and the complex, deep feelings involved.

Time for my rewatch!
