how to make millions before grandma dies

original title: หลานม่า

year: 2024

director: Pat Boonnitipat

quick thoughts

This feels close to a very familiar Asian experience: taking care of old people, the emotions attached to that care, and the strange pressure of starting to show affection only when you realize their presence may not last much longer.

What stayed with me is how the film understands both kinds of people around that situation: those who love unconditionally, and those whose love may be mixed with other motives, without rushing to judge them too easily.

minimal by design. 1mb club mindset.
