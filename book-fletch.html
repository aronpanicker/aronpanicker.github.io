book review: fletch

author: Gregory Mcdonald

year: 1974

quick thoughts

I read Fletch after first watching Confess, Fletch (2022). Jon Hamm feels like perfect casting for I. M. Fletcher: charismatic, funny, and always a little slippery.

The novel itself is a very good mystery with a strong central character. The dialogue is sharp and fun, and the book moves like a quick, off-the-shelf thriller with humor, suspense, and just enough attitude to keep it lively.

It also clearly shows its age. Some of the gender politics feel dated and sexist now, even if that reflects the time in which it was written.

Still, it is a good read if you want something fast, entertaining, and full of personality.
