movie review: marty supreme

year: 2025

director: Josh Safdie

quick thoughts

Timothee is excellent here. The film plays like an Uncut Gems-style sports drama, full of stress and forward momentum.

Even with that intensity, the runtime never feels imposing. The scenes flow easily from one to the next, which keeps the film moving even when it is trying to overwhelm you.

spoilers

The ending did not work for me. The version of the character I had been following throughout the film felt disconnected from the person in the final scene.

minimal by design. 1mb club mindset.
