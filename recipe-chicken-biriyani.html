method

  1. Marinate the chicken with ginger-garlic paste, turmeric powder, coriander powder, 1 teaspoon chilli powder, 1 teaspoon garam masala, 1 teaspoon salt, and lemon juice. Rest for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Thinly slice the onions. Deep fry half of them until golden and crisp. In 1 tablespoon ghee, fry the cashew nuts and raisins separately and set aside.
  3. Heat 3 tablespoons oil in a pot. Add 1 cinnamon stick, 4 cloves, 2 cardamom pods, star anise, mace, 1 bay leaf, and the slit green chilli. Add the remaining onions and saute until soft.
  4. Add the crushed ginger-garlic-green chilli mix, 1/2 teaspoon chilli powder, 1 teaspoon garam masala, and crushed black pepper. Cook briefly, then add the marinated chicken and cook until the chicken is coated well in the masala.
  5. Add 3/4 cup hot water and the coriander-mint leaves. Cover and cook until the chicken is done and the gravy is thick.
  6. Wash the basmati rice. In a separate pot, bring 3 cups water to a boil with 1 bay leaf, 1 cinnamon stick, 4 cloves, 2 cardamom pods, lime juice, 1 tablespoon oil, and 1 teaspoon salt.
  7. Add the rice and cook covered until the water is absorbed and the rice is just done. Rest briefly, then fluff with a fork.
  8. For the dum, spread the chicken masala in the pot and layer the rice on top. Add the fried onions, fried cashews, raisins, coriander leaves, and 2 tablespoons ghee.
  9. Cover tightly and cook on very low heat for 10 to 15 minutes. Rest for 10 minutes before opening and gently mixing.
