c'mon c'mon

year: 2021

director: Mike Mills

quick thoughts

I was grinning through most of this movie.

It is one of those perfect films for black and white. There are so many quotable lines in it, and the whole thing feels warm in a very direct way.

It is a film about companionship and compassion, and it carries both with a lot of tenderness.

I am a sucker for movies that explore the territory of understanding kids, coming of age, and relationships with parents, so this was right up my alley.

Blah. Blah. Blah. Blah!
